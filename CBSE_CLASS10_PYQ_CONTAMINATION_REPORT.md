## Exclusions applied by policy

- Mathematics Basic (241 / 430 series) — excluded, including the 16 above.
- Compartment and Second Board papers — `*-COMPTT/X/*` links were never
  downloaded; the acquisition script rejects them before any request is made.
- Sample papers and marking schemes — not present in these archives; the
  classifier screens for them regardless.
- Mathematics Basic archives (`241_Mathematics_Basic.zip`, `Math_B.zip`, etc.)
  were present on the page and deliberately not requested.

## Language variants

Punjabi-medium variants of the 2022 and 2023 papers are present in the official
archives. They are the same examination and are retained as `language=Punjabi`
in the inventory, not treated as contamination.
