# CBSE Class 10 PYQ — Original Pattern Alignment Analysis

Scope: 188 accepted main-examination PDFs across 10 official
archives (Mathematics Standard 041 and Science 086, 2022–2026), after
quarantining 16 Mathematics Basic files found inside the
official 2026 Mathematics Standard archive.

This analysis is original and structural. It contains no CBSE question text.

## Observations relevant to the 326 draft questions under SME review

1. **2022 is not comparable to the current pattern.** The 2022 papers are
   40-mark term-format papers (median 11–12 pages). The 2023–2026 papers are
   80-mark single-examination papers (median 23–31 pages). Reviewers should
   treat 2023–2026 as the pattern baseline and 2022 as historical only.
2. **Paper length is stable from 2023 onward** — Mathematics settles at 23–24
   pages and Science at 27–31 pages per set, consistent with a fixed
   section A–E structure across those years.
3. **Set breadth is high.** Each year publishes 16–24 distinct sets/series per
   subject, so the diagnostic pool must be robust to set-level variation rather
   than tuned to one paper.
4. **Science grew in 2026** (median 31 pages vs 27 in 2023–2025), the largest
   year-on-year structural movement in the window. Science reviewers should
   check that the 91 Science drafts carry enough multi-part case-study load.
5. **Mathematics Standard vs Basic must stay separated at source.** The 2026
   packaging defect shows the official archive itself can mix them; any future
   ingestion must classify by paper content, never by archive name.

## Limitations

- `Maximum Marks` was read from the first page only; bilingual layouts leave it
  unstated for some sets and one 2024 Mathematics batch reports a truncated
  `8` from spaced glyphs. This affects reporting only, not classification.
- Structure was measured from page and header metadata, not from parsed
  question bodies, because question text is deliberately not imported.
- No claim of question-level similarity to the 326 drafts is made here; that
  determination belongs to the named SMEs.
